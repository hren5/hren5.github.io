About

I am an Associate Professor of Information Systems and Operations Management at the Costello College of Business, George Mason University, where I also coordinate the PhD in Business (Operations and Supply Chain Management concentration). I serve as an Associate Editor for Service Science.

I study healthcare payment design, sustainable business models, AI- and data-driven supply chains, and the operations and marketing interface. I am particularly interested in the role of consumer and employee behavior in shaping both firm profitability and broader environmental and social impact.

I received my PhD in Operations Management from University College London in 2018, following an MRes in Management Science and Innovation there and a combined BSc/MSc in Electrical Engineering from Xi'an Jiaotong University.
